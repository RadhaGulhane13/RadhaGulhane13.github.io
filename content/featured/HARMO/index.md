Is accuracy alone sufficient to guide reinforcement learning? How can we address non-verifiable, out-of-distribution, generalizable task associations along with a verifiable, confidence-aware scorer in policy optimization? We present a hybrid reward modeling optimization that combines (i) model-based rewards, and (ii) rule-based rewards derived from domain-specific heuristics with confidence estimates. By moving beyond accuracy, we introduce multi-aspect rewards that stabilize training and further enhance reasoning ability.<br>
